# Production plan

## Brief

Build a complete, carefully researched digital museum that Elmar can explore without participating in production. The authorized result includes this project directory, a public personal GitHub repository, regular commits and pushes, and no additional GitHub contributors or collaborators.

## Collection

Nine objects, each with an original illustration, a dedicated address, a historical narrative, an interactive mechanism, source annotations, and an honest account of the model's limits:

1. Acoustic mirrors: direction, reflection, and the limits of listening.
2. Mechanical television: sequential scanning and spatial resolution.
3. Pneumatic mail: pressure difference, capsules, and infrastructure.
4. Antikythera mechanism: gear ratios and astronomical cycles.
5. Jacquard loom: editable punched cards and repeated woven patterns.
6. Theremin: touch-free control and heterodyning.
7. Harmonograph: oscillations, frequency ratios, and damping.
8. Zoetrope: discrete frames and sampled motion.
9. Magic lantern: lenses, focus, and projection.

## Design direction

- **Palette:** ink blue #182c4c; deep blue #10213a; vellum #f0e5cc; tarnished brass #c7a465; sea glass #79b9b1; rose pigment #cf8a7f.
- **Type:** self-hosted Fraunces for headings and exhibit titles, Manrope for body and controls. Turkish character coverage is mandatory.
- **Composition:** a physical blue cabinet with individually drawn objects; generous, left-aligned typography; alternating museum displays and hands-on worktables. Objects, materials, light, and their mechanisms carry the visual character.
- **Motion:** explicit operation of each mechanism; no decorative scroll animation; pause/step/reset; reduced-motion support.
- **Access:** strong visible focus, semantic controls, responsive reading widths, captions and numerical alternatives to visual models, optional audio.

## Evidence policy

Museum catalogue inconsistencies and contested dates must be visible in editorial notes. Attribution follows each source's actual scope; one source's national first must not become a worldwide first. Mechanical and visual models are conceptual unless explicitly described otherwise. Original mathematical derivations are separate from historical claims.
