Original variable font files are self-hosted. Exact download URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in [provenance.json](assets/fonts/provenance.json). `python scripts/acquire-fonts.py` refreshes them from the official Google Fonts repository; it is not required for development or deployment.

## Research and illustrations

Museum and research pages are cited as factual references in the application. Descriptions are original paraphrases, not reproductions of catalogue entries. Full third-party articles and photographs are not distributed.

All specimen illustrations, the cabinet, the moon and bouncing-ball artwork, and the interactive diagrams were created for this project as vector or Canvas artwork. They are conceptual explanatory illustrations, not measured reconstructions, museum photographs, or historical facsimiles.

The exported harmonograph SVG is calculated from the visitor's parameters and includes those parameters in its metadata.
