# Research and interpretation

Research date: 13 November 2026. The application contains 21 distinct linked sources. It uses collection records, a national heritage register and research report, an original patent, university-hosted scholarship and a scholarly research abstract. The source book records the access level for each item.

## Editorial decisions

| Object | Evidence used | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acoustic mirror | Historic England's Denge report catalogue and indexed report passages; Kilnsea designation record | A 1999 report's 2017 catalogue publication date is not the construction date. The drawn parabola is not a measured Denge cross-section. |
| Mechanical television | Science Museum Group transmitter and receiver records | The transmitter description separately mentions 30 lenses and a 32-line image. The educational 12/24/48-line scan does not reconstruct either apparatus. |
| Pneumatic mail | Smithsonian National Postal Museum canister and network descriptions | Philadelphia 1893 is the first US line, not a claim of the world's first. The institution's indexed text was readable; direct access to the canister page was rejected. |
| Antikythera | Greek Ministry of Culture museum record; Antikythera Mechanism Research Project data and fragment descriptions; Spinellis's university-hosted 2008 IEEE paper | The museum's circa 150–100 BCE date is explicitly probable. The 235/19 model is a calendar relation, not a complete gear train or an eclipse predictor. |
| Jacquard | National Museums Scotland; Smithsonian punch-card collection | 1801 is the demonstrated mechanism date in NMS's account. It is not silently equated with other sources' adoption or development dates. An influence on Babbage is not equivalence to a general-purpose computer. |
| Theremin | Original US1661058A patent; Lemelson–MIT; Science Museum Group biography | Early demonstration, US patent and RCA manufacture are separate events. The catalogue's conflicting 1920/1928 formulations and outdated popular “only touchless instrument” claims are not adopted. |
| Harmonograph | Science Museum Group Newton and Donkin records | Different mechanical forms and disputed invention attribution are preserved. The numerical oscillator is not an acoustic recording or a full pendulum linkage. |
| Zoetrope | National Trust catalogue text indexed before migration; Christine Veras's 2022 research abstract | 1833/1834 appearance is separated from 1867 commercialization. The full journal article was not claimed as read. The National Trust link currently redirects to a migration notice. |
| Magic lantern | National Science and Media Museum curatorial and conservation writing | Nineteenth-century popularity is not the invention date. A thin-lens example is not the full compound optical system of a historical lantern. |

No catalogue images, academic figures or long verbatim source passages are bundled. The original illustrations make shared principles visible without purporting to restore every missing historical detail.

## Educational models

| Model | Implemented calculation | Deliberate exclusions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acoustic reflection | Vector reflection from `x = y²/(4f)` | Diffraction, interference, measured gain, wind, noise and historical range |
| Television | Source-image row sampling and a sequential scan | Exact Nipkow aperture geometry, historical signal shape and receiver optics |
| Capsule | `F = Δp A`, constant friction, constant acceleration over 30 m | Air compressibility, leakage, velocity-dependent drag, bends and terminal impact |
| Calendar | Exact `235/19` numerical ratio for the chosen example | Real astronomical deviations, nonuniform lunar motion and original gearing |
| Card reader | Editable 8×8 selections, repeated over 24 woven rows | Complete weave structure, thread tension and a historical harness map |
| Theremin | Continuous log-frequency control and calculated difference frequency | RF circuit emulation, antenna-field measurement and historical timbre |
| Harmonograph | Two exponentially damped sinusoidal components | Nonlinear pendulum mechanics, real linkage and measured friction |
| Zoetrope | Discrete motion phases plus a continuous-view blur illustration | Full retinal/neural model, physical slit geometry and a strobe |
| Lantern | Thin-lens image distance and two paraxial rays reaching the actual screen | Aberrations, diffraction, compound optics and a physical point-spread function |

Numerical examples are identified as selected values, not measurements of museum objects. The acoustic delay example chooses 343 m/s; the tube chooses a 10 cm diameter, 30 m length and 2 N friction; the lantern chooses an 80 mm focal length. The exported harmonograph records its actual parameters.

## Source access

The public source book is the canonical claim-to-source mapping. Source-access notes state when only indexed text, an abstract, a catalogue entry or report excerpts were read. A link's availability can change after the research date; the museum migration and institutional request rejection were already disclosed at authoring time.

Mathematical invariants are covered by the pure test suite. Responsive rendering and actual control behavior are covered by browser checks; neither establishes archaeological reconstruction accuracy.
